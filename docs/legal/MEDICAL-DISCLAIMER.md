# Medical disclaimer

**HealthCode Analysis is an editorial and technology demonstration. It is not a healthcare provider and does not provide medical advice.**

## Not medical advice

Content on this site, including articles, reviews, summaries, tool outputs and library search results, is for general information and education only. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, does not prescribe, and does not create a clinician–patient relationship. Always seek the advice of a qualified health professional about a medical question. Never disregard or delay professional advice because of something you read here.

**In an emergency, contact your local emergency number immediately.**

## Educational tools

The BMI calculator, eGFR calculator, AF score worksheet, PICO research worksheet, medical prompt studio and image workbench are **educational demonstrations**:

- They have not been validated for clinical deployment on this site.
- The presence of a well-known formula, reference or disclaimer does not establish regulatory clearance (for example under EU MDR or FDA guidance).
- Their outputs must not be used for clinical decisions.
- Inputs are processed in your browser and are not sent to the server by these tools. Still, use fictional or non-sensitive examples, and never enter identifiable patient information or confidential medical images.
- The image workbench does **not** interpret medical images and does not guarantee anonymization.
