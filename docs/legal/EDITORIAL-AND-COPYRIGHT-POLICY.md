# Editorial and copyright policy

## Editorial standards

**Current state:** the imported library is demonstration content. It has **not** received independent medical or product verification and is excluded from search-engine indexing.

Before an article can be marked verified and made indexable (the ACF indexing switch):

1. Check substantive claims against primary sources, and cite them.
2. Confirm the real author and dates. Never invent names, credentials or reviewer titles.
3. Get medical claims reviewed by an appropriately qualified person, and record that review.
4. Disclose any commercial relationship next to the content it affects.
5. Confirm the rights to every image and quotation.
6. Record the approval (who, when, what was checked).

**Prohibited:** fabricated reviews or testimonials, unverifiable "tested by" claims, invented ratings to fill structured data, and hidden sponsorship.

## Corrections

## Copyright complaints and takedown

If you believe material on the site infringes your rights, contact `[OPERATOR COPYRIGHT CONTACT]` with:

1. The work you claim is infringed.
2. The URL of the material.
3. Your contact details.
4. A statement of good-faith belief that the use is unauthorized.
5. A statement that the information is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorized to act for the owner.
6. Your physical or electronic signature.

The demonstration currently hosts no user-generated content. If uploads or comments are added, the operator should register a DMCA designated agent with the US Copyright Office and apply the EU Digital Services Act notice-and-action duties where applicable.
